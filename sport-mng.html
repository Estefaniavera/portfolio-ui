Sports Management Platform

Ongoing project

UX/UI design case study for a digital platform focused on sports team management and performance tracking.

Project Overview

This project aims to design a centralized platform for sports academies and teams, allowing coaches and staff to manage players, schedules, and performance data efficiently.

Current Focus

The project is currently in its early design phase, focusing on information architecture, user flows, and initial UI concepts.

Planned Features

  • Team and player management
  • Performance and attendance tracking
  • Basic analytics and dashboards
  • Role-based access for coaches and administrators

This case study is currently in progress and will be updated as the project evolves.
