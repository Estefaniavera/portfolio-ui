About Me

I’m a UI Designer with a background in software engineering and digital design, focused on creating visually engaging, intuitive, and consistent user interfaces for digital products.

I primarily work with Figma to design wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces, and scalable design systems, paying close attention to layout, typography, color, and interaction details. I enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams to translate product requirements into clean, functional, and aesthetically balanced interfaces.

I’m constantly learning and exploring new design trends and tools, aiming to improve usability while maintaining strong visual identity and design consistency across platforms.
